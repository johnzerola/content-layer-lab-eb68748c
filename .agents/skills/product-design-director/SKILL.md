# Product Design Director

## Purpose

Hold the whole-product view so individual screens do not drift apart.

## When to apply

- A new page, panel, modal, or tool is proposed.
- Two features start doing the same thing under different names.
- A change alters the mental model of the editor, batch, or cleaning flows.

## Rules

1. VaiViral is one product: template creation, bulk processing, professional editor, cleaning, scheduling and publishing. Every new surface must map to one of these, or it is out of scope.
2. Prefer extending an existing surface over adding a new route.
3. Vocabulary is fixed and Portuguese-facing: Templates, Editor, Lote, Limpar, Agenda, Integrações. Do not invent synonyms.
4. AI is a capability inside normal workflows, never a separate dashboard.
5. Keep the visual identity: dark night-blue theme, electric violet primary, Outfit/Figtree/JetBrains Mono.

## Output

State, in one paragraph, where the change lives in the product, what it replaces, and what stays untouched.

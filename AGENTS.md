## Product UI work

For VaiViral UI/UX, styling and editor interaction tasks, read
`.agents/skills/vaiviral-product-design/SKILL.md` and follow its scoped guidance.
Impeccable is available at `.agents/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md` for visual design.
Do not apply design workflows to unrelated backend/infrastructure-only requests.

## Cleaner engineering intelligence

For Cleaner reconstruction, temporal correspondence, quality, GPU performance,
or clean-room external-system analysis, read
`docs/cleaner-engineering/CLEANER_ENGINEERING_CONTEXT.md` and route work through
`docs/cleaner-engineering/SPECIALIST_ROUTING.md`.

Use at most two specialists for ordinary work and three only when a real
cross-stage boundary requires it. Keep the quality specialist independent and
normally read-only. Do not activate every Cleaner skill or allow parallel agents
to edit the same files.

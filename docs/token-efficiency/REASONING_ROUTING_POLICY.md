# Reasoning routing policy

- Mechanical, reversible, test-backed work starts at low effort.
- Normal implementation starts at medium effort.
- Complex diagnosis starts at high effort.
- Highest effort requires a recorded critical ambiguity or risk.

Reasoning effort and model tier are independent controls. A strong model does not imply highest reasoning. Increase one control at a time where possible, record the escalation cause, and stop after a passing quality gate.

# Creative-editor UX research

Research principles: basic actions must be discoverable and reversible; advanced controls belong in contextual disclosure; direct manipulation needs visible selection, drag feedback, snap feedback and an escape/undo path. These are hypotheses to test in this editor, not a copied product interface.

Initial pattern cards: [timeline trimming](patterns/timeline-trimming.md), [toolbar hierarchy](patterns/toolbar-hierarchy.md), [loading feedback](patterns/loading-feedback.md), [undo](patterns/undo-pattern.md).
